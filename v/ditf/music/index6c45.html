PROFILE

Born in 1982 in Saitama Prefecture. Lyricist, composer, arranger, musician, music producer, guitarist for the folk duo "TANEBI." Achieved a million seller with Nogizaka46's "Sayonara no Imi" and won the Composition Award at the 59th Japan Record Awards in December 2017 for songs like Leo Ieiri's "Zutto, Futari de."
