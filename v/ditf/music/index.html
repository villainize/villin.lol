PROFILE

Born in 1983 in Kagoshima Prefecture. Since her debut in 2001, she has gained popularity with overwhelming visuals and vocals, releasing hit songs such as "Yuki no Hana" and "GLAMOROUS SKY". She is a unique artist who is active not only in Japan but also internationally, in fields such as movies, dramas, and fashion. In recent years, she has energetically expanded her live activities in various genres, attracting attention with her remarkable performance skills. Additionally, she has successfully held solo concerts in Taiwan and China, gaining popularity in Asia.
