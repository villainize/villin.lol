Strelitzia
The Franxx piloted by Hiro and Zero Two.
It fights with a giant spear-like weapon called "Queen Pike," specialized for battling klaxosaurs.

Delphinium
The Franxx piloted by Goro and Ichigo.
It serves as the team's leader, wielding dual weapons known as "Enbishops" in a dual-wielding style.

Argentea
The Franxx piloted by Zorome and Miku.
It specializes in close combat and speed, using claw-like weapons called "Nightcrows" attached to both fists.

Genista
The Franxx piloted by Mitsuru and Kokoro.
It is heavily armored and wields a gun-sword type cannon called "Lux Spada" for devastating close-range attacks.

Chlorophytum
The Franxx piloted by Futoshi and Ikuno.
It focuses on long-range support attacks and can perform extended flight with wings attached to both arms.

Mass Production Model
Franxx used by the 26 units for mass production.
Due to the lack of individual differences, they excel in organized strategic operations.

Type 9
Franxx exclusively for the 9's.
It fights with a spear-type weapon called "Amazon Pike."
