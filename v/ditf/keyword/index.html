KEYWORD

Darling in the Franxx Keyword Glossary

Here we will explain the technical terms used in
"Darling in the Franxx. "

  • APE

    The institution that governs humanity.

  • Yellow blood cells

    A chemical component found in children's blood that allows them to connect with Franxx as a parasite.

  • Adults/Children

    In this world of advanced human beings, all children are called by code numbers.

  • Kissing

    The transfer of magma fuel between plantations
    is often dangerous, as it can attract Klaxosaurs that are after the energy.

  • Klaxosaur （ Dinosaur ）

    A general term for giant creatures that attack humans.

  • Klaxosaur （ Dinosaur ） Size

    Klaxosaurs are classified and identified by names based on their size; specifically, small: Conrad-class
    , medium: Mohorovicic-class
    , large: Gutenberg-class

  • Connect Value （ Paracapacity ）

    A number indicating the degree of synchronization between the pilot and the aircraft aboard the Franxx.

  • Stampede Mode

    This refers to the situation when a Franxx leaves the pilot's control, changes shape, and goes out of control.

  • 13th Plantation
    "Cerussus"

    The plantation where the protagonist's unit, the 13th Squad, is assigned.

  • Parasite

    A general term for those who pilot and ride Franxx.

  • Pistil/Stamen

    The female pilot is called a Pistil and the male pilot is called a Staymen.

  • Franks

    A robot weapon created by humans to fight against the Klaxosaurs.

  • Positive Pulse
    /Negative Pulse

    When piloting a Franxx, the pilot needs to synchronize with the Franxx, and this is the name given to the signals emitted by both parties for this purpose.

  • Magma Fuel

    It is a clean, versatile energy source extracted from underground and powers the fruits of human scientific and technological civilization.

  • Mistilteinn

    Commonly known as Torikago, this is the area within the plantation where the children live.
