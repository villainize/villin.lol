STORY

After parting ways with his friends, Hiro heads for the end of the universe with Strelizia Apas in order to settle the final battle with VIRM.
Continuing to fly through the pitch-black space of space, which is almost like nothingness, Hiro's journey, which seems like an eternity, eventually causes him to lose even his sense of time.
Meanwhile, on Earth, the children who have returned from space are immersed in their lives as they prepare for their future.
The restoration of Mistletin, the awakening of the children who had been put into cold sleep, and the birth of a new life. They tried to become "adults" by repeating partings and encounters, confirming each other step by step.
