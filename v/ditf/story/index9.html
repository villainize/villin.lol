STORY

The children of Unit 13 receive presents from their father. It was a custom that if you told your child what you wanted in advance, your father would deliver it to you once a year.
As each of them rejoices over the gifts they received, Hiro notices that Goro has a hair clip. However, it is not something he received this time, but something he tried to give to Ichigo a long time ago but was unable to give it to him.
At that time, a screaming dragon attacks. During the battle, Delphinium is taken into Kyouryu's body. Goro helped Ichigo escape, but he was left behind in the cockpit...
